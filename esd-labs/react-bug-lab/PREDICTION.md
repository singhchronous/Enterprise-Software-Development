# My prediction

Name: ……………………  Roll number: ……………………  Variant: ______

**Parts 1–4 come first.** Write them from `TRIGGER.md` and the code in `src/` — *before*
you start the app, and *before* you open any AI tool. (Run `npm install` while you read;
just don't `npm run dev` yet.) Hand parts 1–4 in the way your TA announces. Then start the
app, trigger, fix, re-trigger, and fill in parts 5–9.

---

## 1. What I think is wrong

The cause, in one or two sentences, not the symptom. ("The list doesn't update" is the
symptom; *why* doesn't it update?)



## 2. Where it is

File and line, or the component, hook or function. If you're torn between two places,
name both and say which one you'd bet on.



## 3. What I will see when I run the trigger

Be specific, and say something the bug report doesn't: what the Network tab will show,
what the console will say, what happens if you then do one more thing.



## 4. My fix, and what the trigger will show after it

The change you will make, and what the same steps will show afterwards.



---

*Hand in parts 1–4 now. Everything below is written after.*

---

## 5. What actually happened when I ran the trigger

Did part 3 come true? What surprised you?



## 6. What I changed

The lines before and after (or paste the diff).



## 7. Re-trigger

Same steps, after the fix. What did you see? Did anything else break?



## 8. Why it happened

One or two sentences, in your own words, that would convince a classmate.



## 9. Looking back

Was your prediction right? If not, what fooled you? If you used AI after part 4: what
did you ask, what did it say, and where was it right or wrong?
